Frequently Asked

Questions About Pricing

Do you offer discounts for recurring bookings?
Yes! We offer 10% off for weekly bookings and 5% off for bi-weekly service. Discounts are applied automatically when you set up recurring service.
What is your cancellation policy?
We ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations. Cancellations made within 24 hours may be subject to a small fee to cover the team's reserved time.
Are your products eco-friendly?
We offer eco-friendly and pet-safe product options on all services. Simply let us know your preference at the time of booking.
What if I'm not satisfied with my clean?
We stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If anything was missed, contact us within 24 hours and we'll return to make it right at no charge.
How do I pay?
We accept all major credit cards, e-transfer, and cash. Payment is due at time of service for one-time cleans. Recurring clients are billed after each visit.
