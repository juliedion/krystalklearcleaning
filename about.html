Our Story

From One Home
to Hundreds

Krystal Klear Clean was founded by Becky with a simple mission: provide exceptional, reliable cleaning services that families and businesses can truly count on.

What started as a single-person operation has grown into a trusted team of skilled cleaning professionals covering residential, commercial, rental, and specialty cleaning needs. Through every stage of that growth, we've never lost the personal touch that sets us apart.

We invest in our team, our training, and our community — because we believe the best results come from people who genuinely care about the work they do.
