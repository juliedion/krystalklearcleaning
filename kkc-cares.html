KKC Cares

When life gets hard, a clean home shouldn't be one more thing to worry about. KKC Cares gives free professional cleanings to neighbors going through cancer treatment, serious illness, and other traumatic life events.

Our Mission

Cleaning with
Conscience

KKC Cares is our own community giving program, run by our team for our neighbors. A clean space can change how you feel, rest, and recover, and we believe that should be within reach for anyone going through the hardest moments of life.

Every month, we set aside two free cleanings for people undergoing cancer treatment. We also keep a waitlist for anyone in our community facing a serious illness, a loss, or another traumatic event, and we proudly support local organizations doing important work close to home.

"A clean space is more than just tidy. It's a foundation for dignity, clarity, and fresh starts."
— Becky, Founder

The KKC Cares Waitlist

Going Through
Something Hard?

If you or someone you love is facing cancer treatment, a serious illness, a loss, or another traumatic event, we'd be honored to help. Join our waitlist and we'll reach out as cleaning spots open up.

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Fill out the short form for yourself, or nominate someone you know.

  2. 2

    Share a doctor's note

    If the application is for an illness, we ask for a doctor's note confirming the diagnosis or treatment.

  3. 3

    We review and reach out

    Our team reviews every application personally and keeps all information confidential.

  4. 4

    Your free clean is scheduled

    As spots open, we'll contact you to book a time that works for you.

Waitlist Application

All information is kept private and used only to arrange your cleaning.

Who is this application for?

A doctor's note is required for illness-related applications. Once we review your application, we'll let you know how to send it to us privately.

Submitting this form adds you to our waitlist. It doesn't guarantee a cleaning date, and we'll be in touch as spots become available.
